# First floor

`chubaka/floorplan.py` describes the first floor from the evacuation plan. Room 08 is the guard room and keeps its hand-made models. Everything else, including the corridors and the central hall, is generated by `chubaka/building.py`.

Coordinates in the data tables use plan directions: `e` grows to the east (right on the plan) and `n` to the north (up). The game world uses the same numbers: `x = e` and `z = n`. The engine is left-handed (`+x` is to the right when you face `+z`), so the map is not mirrored: standing in the guard door and facing south, east is on your left.

The inner courtyards are empty pits that only show through windows. The assembly hall with its vestibule and all staircases are not part of the game map.

The central hall (`lobby`) lies between the two courtyards, directly in front of the guard door. It has four square columns (`COLUMN_DATA`) and narrows into the pink central passage (`channel`) between the two upper toilets. The passage is a plain 3 m corridor: only the toilets have doors on it, and rooms 17 and 18 beside it are solid.

## Editing the map

| Table | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `REGION_DATA` | Rooms and corridors as rectangles of floor |
| `LINK_DATA` | Doors between two neighbouring regions and the position along the wall |
| `GAP_DATA` | Fully open passages between corridors |
| `EXIT_DATA` | Doors to the outside |
| `NODE_DATA` | Points where Echoes can stand |
| `EDGE_DATA` | Which points are connected |
| `CAMERA_DATA` | The laptop cameras: label such as 1A, title, position, compass heading |
| `AUDIO_CAMERA_DATA` | Cameras without a picture: label, title, position, heading, and the room they listen to |
| `COLUMN_DATA` | Centres of the hall columns |
| `WINDOW_DATA` | Windows cut into walls that face the courtyards |

Walls are added around every region automatically. `tests/test_floorplan.py` checks that every edge between points is not blocked by a wall and that every point can reach the guard door.

## Cameras

Cameras are listed in `CAMERA_DATA`. Add a row, and the laptop gets a new marker and a place in the list. The map panel shows the view cone of the selected camera, clipped by walls and columns, with an arrow along its axis. Every video camera has a small pointer on its marker.

A camera in `AUDIO_CAMERA_DATA` has no picture: the screen shows only noise, the map highlights the room it listens to, and the sounds of Echoes inside that room (steps, creaks, the door) are played. It never sees anything, so abilities such as `CameraFreeze` ignore it.

Every numbered room gets a plate with its number above the door on the corridor side, and every corridor has its own floor tint, so cameras are easy to tell apart. Keys 1 to 9 select the first nine, A and D and the arrow buttons on the panel cycle through all.
